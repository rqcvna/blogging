# International Humanitarian Law (IHL)

## Key Principles of IHL

- **Principle of Distinction:** Parties to a conflict must distinguish between combatants and civilians.
- **Principle of Proportionality:** Incidental harm to civilians must not be excessive relative to the military advantage.
- **Principle of Precaution:** Precautions must be taken in attacks and against the effects of attacks.
